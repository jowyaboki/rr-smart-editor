# Roadmap v6.0 — Global Scales and Connectivity

Strategic targets for upcoming releases under the v6.x and next-generation v7.0 connected ecosystem lifecycle.

## 1. Phase 1: Mobile Companion Deploy (Q1 2026)
- **App Store Deployments**: Publish official companion apps to the iOS App Store and Google Play.
- **Push Alert Infrastructure**: Establish Apple APNs and Google FCM pipelines.

## 2. Phase 2: Decentralized Rendering (Q2 2026)
- **Peer-to-Peer Rendering Nodes**: Allow studio users to optionally share idle workstation rendering power over a secure P2P mesh network.
- **Dynamic Workload Splitting**: Advanced frame slice sharding algorithms.

## 3. Phase 3: Global Media Knowledge Graph (Q3 2026)
- **Cross-Enterprise Semantic Relations**: Leverage graph databases to detect similar visual styles, color profiles, and audio pacing across discrete international organizations.
