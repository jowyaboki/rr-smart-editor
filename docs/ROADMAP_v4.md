# Roadmap v4.0

Strategic direction and planned platform enhancements for the upcoming MAJOR version release.

## 1. Collaborative Editing Engine (Q1 2026)
- **OT Collaborative Engine**: Real-time multi-editor timeline conflict resolution using Operational Transformations.
- **Presence Indicators**: Visual tracking of active workspace collaborators.

## 2. Advanced GPU Clustering & Splitting (Q2 2026)
- **Scene-Level Sharding**: Automatically segment video timelines into distinct scene buckets, distributing the workload across horizontal cluster nodes for simultaneous rendering.
- **Dynamic Cost Optimizer**: Smart auto-scaling nodes matched against cost-budget limits.

## 3. Generative AI Video Inpainting (Q3 2026)
- **Object Inpainting**: Smart mask-and-fill model integrations directly accessible via the Timeline Effects panel.
- **Automated Scene Enhancer**: Intelligent audio-visual tone matching.
