# Support Policy

Support services and SLAs for commercial deployments of the RR Smart Editor Studio Platform (v3.0).

## 1. Ticket Severities and SLA Targets

| Severity | Description | Response SLA | Target Resolution |
|----------|-------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **S1 (Blocker)** | Complete production outage; rendering services down. | < 1 Hour | < 4 Hours |
| **S2 (High)** | Major feature down; local previews failing. | < 4 Hours | < 24 Hours |
| **S3 (Normal)**| General workflow query or minor UI bug. | < 24 Hours | < 3 Days |

## 2. Communication Channels

Commercial clients can log incidents via:
- **Admin Console Ticketing System**
- **SLA Escalation Hotline**
- **Commercial Support Slack/Teams Portal**
