# Brief Builder Guide

## Overview
The `CreatorBriefBuilder` converts raw creator ideas into structured `ProductionBrief` specifications.

## Brief Parameters
- **Title**: Production project name.
- **Prompt**: Raw creative vision or story prompt.
- **Target Platform**: YouTube (16:9), TikTok (9:16), Instagram Reels (9:16), Broadcast HD (16:9).
- **Target Duration**: Expected video length in seconds.
- **Quality Score Threshold**: Required quality gate threshold score (50-100).
