# Business Intelligence Guide

This guide describes executive analytics and growth indicators tracked in the RR Smart Editor Studio Platform (v5.0).

## 1. Growth Metrics

The executive business intelligence (BI) dashboard tracks several financial and engagement indicators:
- **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)**: Calculated on active subscription models.
- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**: Cohort-based monthly billing tracking.
- **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of users moving from Free to Pro/Studio tiers.
- **Activation Rate**: Percentage of workspaces completing onboarding and triggering their first export within 7 days.
- **Retention Cohorts**: Track user engagement month-over-month.

## 2. Infrastructure Cost Analytics

To ensure healthy margins, dashboards compare server infrastructure overheads against subscription pricing:
- **Cloud Storage & Database Costs**: Tracked per organization workspace.
- **GPU Render Costs**: Calculated based on SD vs HD compute hours.
- **AI Token Costs**: Computational spend on Whisper transcription and script generation APIs.
