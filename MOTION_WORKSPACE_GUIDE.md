# Motion Workspace Guide

The Motion Graphics Workspace is a dedicated creative workspace designed to bridge timeline elements with premium, physics-informed animation keyframes.

## Layer Management & Composition Hierarchy
* **Layers Panel**: Access layers, watermarks, text blocks, and sequences stacked chronologically.
* **Easing Library Presets**: Choose from Linear, Ease In, Ease Out, and Bezier S-Curve presets to calculate dynamic frame transitions.
* **Transform Alignments**: One-click alignment guides for snap centering or edge bounding.
