# Long-Term Support (LTS) Policy - Phase 10 (Product Evolution)

The long-term support policy outlining deprecation boundaries.

## 1. Release Support Matrix
* **Stable Releases**: Supported for 12 months with security patches.
* **LTS Releases**: Supported for 24 months with regular maintenance updates.
* **API Deprecation**: All API deprecations require a 6-month pre-announcement period.
